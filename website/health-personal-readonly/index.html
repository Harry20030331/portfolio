Health Personal Readonly

A personal desktop tool operated by Yuming Feng to read his own Google Health records and review activity, exercise, sleep, and wellness trends.

Access and use

The tool requests read-only access to activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements. It does not create, change, or delete Google Health records. Selected records may be used in owner-directed analysis, including with an AI assistant, and to prepare personal wellness journal drafts. This public website provides information only; it does not retrieve or display health records.
