Health Personal Readonly — Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Purpose and data access

Yuming Feng operates this tool for personal use. With the account owner’s consent, it reads Google Health activity and fitness, sleep, and health metrics and measurements. These records support personal activity summaries, wellness reflection, and owner-directed analysis.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in private files on the owner’s computer. Records may be processed in memory or saved locally when needed for the requested task. Selected data may be provided to an AI assistant or included in a document at the owner’s direction. Such services have their own privacy and retention policies. The tool does not automatically publish health records or submit them to a school or other recipient.

The tool does not sell health data or use it for advertising. Google Health data is not used by this tool to train a general-purpose AI model. No health records or credentials are included on this public website.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke this tool’s access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future API access; it does not erase existing exported files or prior assistant conversations. The owner can delete local exports and credentials, and manage any conversation or document copies through the relevant service.
